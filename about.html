About Us

Water is Life — We Bring It to the Surface

Based in Giyani, Limpopo, Kokwani Chauke Water Drilling is a trusted name in professional borehole drilling. We provide complete water solutions — from drilling to pump installation — ensuring communities and households have access to clean, reliable water.

Our experienced team uses modern drilling equipment to deliver quality results on time and within budget. Every full package includes PVC piping, pump installation, control box and steel casing — everything you need for a functional borehole.

  • Experienced & professional teamSkilled drillers who treat your property with respect.
  • Modern drilling equipmentReliable rigs that drill faster and cleaner.
  • Full packages – no hidden costsOne price, everything included, no surprises.
  • Serving Giyani & surrounding areasMalamulele, Lulekani, Thohoyandou and beyond.

Our Journey

From One Rig to Hundreds of Boreholes

A story written in water — serving the people of Limpopo year after year.

The Beginning

Founded in Giyani

Kokwani Chauke started with a single drilling rig and one mission: bring affordable, clean water to families across Limpopo.

Growth

Modern Equipment, Bigger Reach

Investment in modern truck-mounted rigs allowed faster mobilisation to remote villages — from Malamulele to Lulekani and beyond.

Today

Full-Package Water Solutions

Today we deliver complete turn-key boreholes: drilling, PVC and pump installation, control boxes and steel casing — all at transparent fixed prices.

The Future

Water for Every Household

Our goal is simple: no family in our service area should go without clean, reliable water. "A va borhi mati manga humi."
