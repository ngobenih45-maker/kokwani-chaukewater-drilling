What We Offer

Complete Borehole Solutions

Everything you need for a complete borehole solution, from drilling to pump installation.

01

Borehole Drilling

Professional drilling from 40m to 100m depth using advanced truck-mounted equipment for a reliable water supply. We assess your site, drill with precision and develop the borehole until it delivers clean water.

02

PVC & Pump Installation

Complete pump and PVC pipe installation included in all our full packages. We size the pump to your borehole yield and household needs so you never run dry.

03

Steel Casing

Durable steel casing to protect your borehole against collapsing soil and contamination. 1m included in every package — extras available at R700/m.

04

Control Box Setup

Electrical control box installation for seamless pump operation and management. Protects your pump from surges and makes switching effortless.

How It Works

From First Call to Flowing Water

A simple, proven process refined over hundreds of boreholes.

Step 1

Call or WhatsApp Us

Tell us your location and water needs. We advise on the ideal depth and package — free of charge.

Step 2

Site Assessment & Quotation

We visit your property, assess access and geology, then give you a fixed full-package price. No hidden costs.

Step 3

Drilling Day

Our rig arrives and drills to the agreed depth, installing steel casing where the formation requires it.

Step 4

Pump, PVC & Control Box

We install the pump, PVC pipes and control box, then test the system until clean water flows reliably.
