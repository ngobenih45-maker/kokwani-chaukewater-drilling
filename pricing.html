Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you book.

Every full package includes borehole drilling to the agreed depth, PVC piping, pump installation, a control box, black pipe and 1m of steel casing. One fixed price — no hidden costs.

Each package includes 1m of steel casing. Additional steel casing is charged at R700 per metre. A standalone 1m steel casing section is R1 500.

Most household boreholes are completed within 2 to 3 days, depending on depth, ground conditions and site access. We confirm a realistic timeline with your quotation.

We are based in Giyani and serve surrounding areas including Malamulele, Lulekani, Thohoyandou, Tzaneen, Phalaborwa, Hoedspruit and the wider Limpopo region.

As a guide: 40–50m suits small households, 60–70m covers most family homes, and 80–100m is best for farms and high water demand. Call us for free site-specific advice.
